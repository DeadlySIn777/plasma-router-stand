### Quote scope that makes comparisons useful

Request separate figures for six full 20-foot bars, sales tax, and delivery to Alto 30510. Specify 2 × 2 × 0.120-inch nominal wall, uncoated steel, requested ASTM A500 grade, and request that any offered A513 or 0.125-inch substitute be identified before acceptance. Ask for cash/small-order eligibility, minimum order, stock availability, length tolerance, and lead time. Quote any transport cuts separately; do not cut the six optimized bars at arbitrary halfway points because the existing cut plan must be checked first. The user performs fabrication, so no welding, assembly, or finish labor is required.

SteelMart is the first local quote candidate based on its Gainesville location; Sabel Winder is an independent delivery-capable competitor. Neither is verified cheapest. No numerical savings or revised build subtotal is credited from these unpriced leads.
