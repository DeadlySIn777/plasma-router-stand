# Chassis cost and owned aluminum

The **$1,392.71 goods** category called Chassis covers the stand, removable bed beams, water-assembly supports, storage supports and structural flat stock. It does not mean the bare stand alone costs that amount. Freight, unpriced sheets, sand and finishing are not all included in this number.

| Priced stock | Goods USD |
|---|---:|
| Six 20-foot bars, 2 × 2 × .120 inch steel tube | 930.90 |
| 1 × 1 inch brace tube | 60.79 |
| Steel rail caps and spacers | 136.28 |
| Three plate purchases for gussets, brackets and feet | 264.74 |
| Total currently sourced chassis-category goods | **1,392.71** |

The six-bar offer is $155.15 per 20-foot bar, equivalent to $7.7575 per purchased foot. It is the existing [BlueSky Supplies posted offer](https://www.blueskysupplies.com/products/2inch-x-2-inch-x-120-a500-tube), not a delivered local SteelMart quotation or a claim that this is the best achievable local price. Owner fabrication labor is $0.

That tube reference is high relative to a matching [Bobco A500 Grade B listing](https://www.bobcometal.com/hot-rolled-steel-square-tube-2-inch-x-0-12.html): $97.50 per 20-foot stick, or $585 for six. The $345.90 goods difference is not a delivered saving; Bobco advertises Los Angeles pickup and Georgia freight is unquoted. [SteelMart Gainesville](https://steelmartatlanta.com/steelmart-gainesville-ga/) and [Sabel Steel Winder](https://www.sabelsteel.com/location/winder-ga/) are regional quote candidates. Sabel advertises delivery within 150 miles of Winder; its price and exact order fulfillment need a quote. See [the full supplier comparison](TUBE-PRICE-COMPARISON.md).

The cut plan consumes 118.03 net feet in 36 tube blanks. Approximately 59.99 feet are the main frame; 26.67 feet are the paired removable bed beams; 14.58 feet are reservoir supports; 10.00 feet support the pan; and 6.80 feet support the storage rack. These are allocations of one combined stock purchase. They are not separate rounded-up bar orders. About half the tube is therefore in the main frame and half in the bed/water/storage system. The footprint-contained conversion mechanism has a real material cost.

The separate Motion category contains **$813.33 of aluminum adapter stock**. The main motion adapters were already modeled in aluminum, including integral thick brackets and supports. That amount is not part of the $1,392.71 chassis number.

## Stock the owner has

The owner reported 1/2-inch and 3/8-inch aluminum with 12 × 12-inch dimensions. Record provisionally **one of each**; quantity and alloy were not separately specified. Do not charge for acquiring those existing pieces. Do not assume their alloy, flatness or usable finished thickness is qualified.

The 1/2-inch piece can accommodate the current Z-carrier blank and several smaller components. Only the **$58.74 MET08 Z-carrier stock purchase** can be avoided independently in the current buying plan; the other small pieces already came from offcuts of a longer stock purchase still required by other parts. The 3/8-inch piece has no direct unchanged aluminum part at its nominal 9.525 mm thickness.

The priced subtotal remains $5,810.24 until the replacement stock is qualified. If the owned 1/2-inch material meets the applicable material and finished-thickness requirements, removing MET08 gives **$5,751.50 for the same incomplete priced scope before tax**, not a complete delivered build total. Supplier shipping may change; no shipping saving is assumed.

See [owned aluminum fit and allocation](OWNED-ALUMINUM-FIT.md). The preference is to use owned aluminum for appropriate mounts. Steel frame weldments, rail datums and bed supports must not receive an unchecked material substitution, and thick integral parts require an actual joint redesign to be made from thinner plate.
