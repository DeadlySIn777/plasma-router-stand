It is a retailer's public price, not evidence that a nearby steel yard will charge the same amount. The frozen requirement is **2 × 2 × .120-inch A500 square tube, six full 20-foot lengths**. No thinner-wall substitution, short-length splice, or grade change has been adopted.

## Procurement conclusion

The high price is at least partly the supplier selection: one exact-spec primary-source offer is 37.2% below the current tube goods line. The better next procurement comparison is a local full-stock A500 quote, including all six tubes and the other steel on the same delivery, against $930.90 plus the current source's unknown freight. Cross-country advertised bargains establish negotiation benchmarks; they do not establish savings after freight.

No suppliers were contacted, no orders were placed, and the cost workbook/CAD were not changed by this comparison.
