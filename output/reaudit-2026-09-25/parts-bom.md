# Parts and BOM re-audit — 25 September 2026

**Disposition: NOT ready to order.** This bounded independent audit compares the current Rev F definition with the purchase and cost records. It makes no implementation changes, contacts no supplier, and does not refresh prices. Existing source observations dated 24 September are evidence records, not new verified offers. Mechanical qualification and control firmware are reviewed separately.

## Confirmed current requirements

- The current bed is **1,000 × 1,197 mm**, ten full-length 100 × 20 profiles, rather than the earlier six cassettes. `MECHANICAL-ENGINEERING.md:12` and `cutlist.json:2625` agree.
- **Five two-packs = ten 1,220 mm bars** is the correct pack arithmetic for this layout. Each bar produces one 1,197 mm strip. At 3 mm saw kerf, 20 mm remains per bar before further end preparation. Total finished length is 11,970 mm from 12,200 mm stock. This confirms quantity, not supplier tolerances or flatness.
- The current square-tube schedule contains **28 blanks / 28,356 mm**, fitting **five 20-foot bars**, with 10 mm trim per bar and 3 mm per cut. Three bars have just 6 mm calculated remainder. This is conditional on actual full usable stock length and the stated kerf.
- The cut list carries 50 M5×25 deck screws, 70 DIN 562 M5 square nuts, 20 M5×20 spoilboard screws, 16 #12-14×32 deck TEKs, four M8×70 drawdowns, two Ø10×56 dowels, and four 938.4 mm 2×1.5×.120 crossmembers. Four separate #12-14×38 cabinet TEKs are also modeled.
