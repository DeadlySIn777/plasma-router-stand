# Current structural joints and assembly qualification

This document supersedes the historical Rev F strength-screen examples of 12 or
6 N·m. They are not current settings for the cassette bed. The forces below are
**qualification preload targets**, conditional on specified steel parts, class
8.8 or stronger screws, sound welds, flat contact and measured actual hardware.
They are not torque-table instructions or recorded physical test results.

| Joint | Preload target per screw | Purpose and limiting features |
|---|---:|---|
| Eight beam M8 drawdowns | 2,000–4,000 N | Sleeves carry compression; positive Ø6 locators carry horizontal load. Re-map seats after assembly. |
| Four removable front-seat M8 screws | 2,000–4,000 N | Two screws clamp each seat to its ledger; verify face contact and the sleeve welds. |
| Sixteen panel clamp M6 screws | 800–1,000 N | Limit bending of the end bridges and local extrusion contact. Ordinary M6 maximum torque is inappropriate. |
| Twenty-four spoilboard M5 screws | Unreleased; qualify initially at 50–75 N only after the test below | Thin slot lips, nut-strip support and MDF contact govern. No unconditional setting is released. |
| M3 strip retainers | Contact adjustment only | Remove longitudinal play without stripping the 2.7 mm strip or indenting the profile. They are not workholding clamps. |
| Panel receiver lower M6 screws | Axial retention, 500–1,000 N target | Ø14 rods are laterally supported in sockets; these screws do not provide cantilever bending fixity. Use M6×20 with 9 mm modeled thread engagement. |

Do not infer these tensions from an arbitrary hand-tightening instruction. Use
an actual-joint calibration with a suitable force-measuring arrangement or a
verified tension method. For illustration only, T=KFd with K=0.20 gives 4.8 N·m
for an M8 at 3,000 N, 1.08 N·m for an M6 at 900 N, and 0.0625 N·m for an M5 at
62.5 N. Actual K depends on finish and lubrication; these example torques do not
establish the requested tensions. Do not apply lubricant after calibration or
use a powered impact driver for these joints.

For the front-seat screws, the minimum assumed coefficient of friction 0.15
and two 2,000 N preloads give 600 N nominal sliding resistance per seat. The
panel calculation credits only two equivalent full clamp loads per panel,
giving 240 N at the 800 N minimum. These margins depend on real surface state;
they are not a substitute for measuring slip. Neither rough paint nor a washer
bridging an unsupported edge is an accepted contact surface.

The beam locators have a separate positive shear check: one Ø6 pin is evaluated
at 500 N without friction, with 4 mm bearing engagement and the actual 6 mm
seat. Nominal pin bending is 94.3 MPa and seat bearing is 20.8 MPa. Match the
6.03 mm round bore to the measured pin, retain the pin in its beam foot, and
keep the opposite locator relieved along X. Front-seat clearance bolts and
panel friction clamps do not establish an automatic repeatable datum.

## Extrusion / nut-strip acceptance

Make one representative section using the actual purchased profile, the
modeled strip, both retainers, actual M5 screw and actual finished MDF. Apply
350 N upward at each screw station, hold 60 seconds, release, and repeat ten
times. Measure displacement independently of the test fixture. Require no
lip cracking, thread damage, strip slip or MDF distress, and no more than
0.02 mm residual movement. Inspect the slot-lip roots after testing. This is a
proposed test; it has not been performed and its pass is not guaranteed.

The nominal lip screen allows only about 180 N combined bolt force for a
twofold yield reserve; use 175 N as the conditional ceiling. A 75 N preload
therefore leaves only 100 N for a local uplift increment. This is why an
ordinary M5 torque-table value is unacceptable. The very small tension also
requires suitable calibration equipment; it is not an instruction to estimate
force by feel.

If the actual coupon passes, calibrate the candidate 50–75 N M5 preload with the actual
hardware. Then verify a full panel/board assembly under a 200 N horizontal
qualification load with no slip or permanent shift above 0.02 mm. Any failure
requires revising the contact/retention detail before use. Do not compensate
by increasing screw torque. Cutting workholding must be independently sized
for the stock and cutter; these screws secure the spoilboard, not arbitrary
workpieces.

## New receiver fabrication

Use Ø14.00 ±0.05 rods with documented yield strength at least 300 MPa. Do not
substitute unidentified scrap. Turn the sockets to OD18.2 ×51 mm; finish the
Ø14.20 +0.05/-0.00 guide bore 40 mm deep from the top **after welding**. The
resulting 0.15–0.30 mm diametral sliding clearance is intentional for a storage
retainer. Exclude coatings from the bore and rod contact lengths. It is not a
precision machining datum.

The 45 ×245 ×8 receiver seats retain top Z175 and extend 5 mm outward to
support the complete underside weld ring. The socket projects 3 mm below the
seat. Make a continuous 3 mm E70 fillet below the seat, then finish the bore;
no weld is placed where it could contact a stored panel. The relieved shelf
web has a 28 ×5 mm notch for the socket/weld and 10.4 mm remaining height.
Check the nominal 0.9 mm socket-to-panel gap and require at least 0.5 mm after
welding. Preserve the seat top plane and the adjacent panel separator datums.

Revised lower M6×20 countersunk screws replace M6×12. Minimum nominal rod thread
engagement is 9 mm. The under-head access above the fork is now 12.4 mm; a
physical check with the actual short hex tool is required. A long hex key is
not assumed to fit.

## Welds and feet

The receiver's real circular weld group is checked using a 3 mm equal-leg
fillet and 70 ksi electrode classification. The calculation uses an AISC ASD
nominal weld-metal screen, not certification of this machine or the actual
weld. Fusion, undercut, distortion, parent steel condition and local base-metal
capacity remain physical acceptance items. Do not weld sealed sand-filled or
wet members. All structural welds and sleeves precede ballast filling.

Adjust all six custom M16 feet into contact, hold the jack while tightening its
jam nut, then re-check frame twist and contact. The 650 kg upper-mass case assigns
one third of weight to a single foot. It does not authorize installing this
machine on an unknown suspended floor. Pad pressure, friction and any floor
anchors must match the actual supporting surface. The calculations do not
credit sand or a purchased-foot load rating.
