## Design and selection notes

- User owns a 12 CFM compressor, venturi valves and solenoids; models and ratings are pending. This file prices the electrically pumped baseline for comparison. A pneumatic air-displacement option requires a separately designed pressure chamber; the custom vented reservoir specified here must not be assumed pressure-rated.
- W01 feedback green-white closes at fully open; yellow-white closes at fully closed per manufacturer. White is signal common, not supply.
- 157.5 L is internal gross volume; 125 L usable capacity requires dimensions/ports/baffle/freeboard validation. 110 L total charge includes all pan, tank and plumbing water.
- No verified drop-in stock reservoir found. Example 40 gal RV tank39x18x14in is990.6x457.2x355.6mm, exceeds nominal900x500x350 internal target and lacks cleaning/baffle details; not selected.
- Pan normal815 vs overflow825 leaves only10mm nominal headroom. Float mounting tolerance, hysteresis, slosh and pump shutoff lag must be checked before selecting sensor positions.
- Approximately80 L pan transfer at a nominal19 L/min pump would take at least4.2minutes at zero-loss nominalflow; actual pump head/fittings make it longer. No guaranteed swap time stated.
- Fluid additives and metal fines require materials and sediment-cycle validation for valve,pump,hoses,seals and sensors. Supplier water/bilge ratings are not abrasive-plasma certification.
- 25mm frame+8mmAl+19mmMDF=52mm panel stack; 920.8+52=972.8mm unskimmedrouterplane. Hardware must fit this exact stack and protectedstorage.
- Retain primary budget valve207.99; Valworx638.40, HSH98.99, CPVC175.90 and manual15.72 are alternatives, never summed together.
