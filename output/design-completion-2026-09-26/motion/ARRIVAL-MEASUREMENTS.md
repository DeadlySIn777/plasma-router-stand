# Small measurement record that finishes the purchased interfaces

Use millimeters. Record the measuring tool and its stated uncertainty. Do not
force a guessed screw into a purchased hole. A photo records the datum/part
identity; it is not used to scale a dimension.

## Y pair - when the already-ordered modules arrive

| Record | Y left | Y right |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full package/module/motor label; 1000 travel and10 lead identity | pending | pending |
| Base width/height, motor projection and cable outlet | pending | pending |
| Base slot centerline(s) from an identified outer edge | pending | pending |
| Supplied nut: thread/pitch, width/thickness, usable screw engagement and insertion route | pending | pending |
| Nonmotor end datum to output center at both usable travel limits; distinguish hard stop from usable limit | pending | pending |
| Output4-hole pattern/thread/depth and output-face height (compare published65.5 height,30 x30 M4 pattern) | pending | pending |

Also record if one full screw revolution moves the output10 mm while unloaded.
Match the two modules before drilling either chassis mount. Keep the output
carriages installed on their guides. The independent HGR guides carry the
gantry moment; the HMS40 modules provide drive thrust through the floating links.

## Z - obtain drawing before order, or measure the purchased100 mm unit

| Datum / interface | Required entry |
| --- | --- |
| Output height H | Distance from actual base mounting face to output mounting face; not just carriage-body thickness |
| Output fixing holes1-4 | For each: X/Y from the same two carriage edges, thread and pitch or plain bore, usable depth, backside access, ownership as accessory fixing hole |
| Base nut interface | Slot centerline, nut geometry/thread, usable engagement, insertion route and flat seating area |
| Motor flange | Mount hole coordinates/diameters, pilot diameter/depth, shaft diameter/projection and coupling type/bore/clamp engagement |
| Stroke datum | Output location at both usable ends, available overtravel, end-block and switch clearances |

The larger guide-assembly bores are not assumed to be accessory fixing holes.
Record the original motor label and keep the coupling undisturbed until the
replacement plan is dimensionally established.

## Existing torch - no model purchase required to take these measurements

1. Diameter of the straight insulated barrel at the actual clamping zone, at
   both ends and in two directions; permitted straight clamp length.
2. Distance along the axis from the nozzle tip to the lower end of that zone,
   plus the largest head diameter below it.
3. Body/lead projection above the clamp and the manufacturer's minimum lead
   bend radius, or the exact lead part number for obtaining it.
4. Torch and source label/connector identity. VIV ARC CUT-50 and AG60-style shape
   do not determine starting method, machine connection or isolated voltage tap.

These measurements define the physical mount. They do not authorize an
unverified torch-start or raw arc-voltage electrical connection.
