# Scrap-yard tube guide — current chassis

Use this to choose usable material. Rev H retains the Rev G 2-inch tube blank lengths; leave scrap stock long until its actual dimensions and condition are checked against the final cutting plan.

**Section:** nominal 2 x 2 inch mild-steel square tube, 0.120 inch wall in the CAD (roughly 1/8 inch). Scrap material has no assumed ASTM grade or remaining-wall guarantee. Measure clean metal at multiple accessible places; paint and rust do not count as wall thickness. Straightness, sound corners and intact wall matter at least as much as outside size.

Total: 30 blanks, 96.69 feet finished. Five full 20-foot sticks fit the present schedule with a 3 mm saw kerf and 10 mm trim per stick. Random scrap lengths need a different nesting plan; equal total footage does not guarantee the cuts fit.

Prioritize four sound pieces at least 58 inches long for the top rails and bed ledgers. Keeping longer stock intact usually gives more nesting choices. Do not splice the top rails or bed beams merely to recover short scrap without a designed joint.

Set aside pieces with deep pitting, holes, buckles, split seams or damaged corners. Surface rust can be cleaned for inspection, but hidden internal corrosion cannot be accepted from appearance alone. Old welded attachments must leave sufficient sound length after removal. Unknown scrap strength and wall condition remain material-verification requirements; the new-stock calculations do not certify scrap.

**Send back:** number of pieces, usable lengths after damaged ends, measured wall thickness, and price. Record the longest straight, undamaged segment in each piece. Do not infer wall thickness from the outside 2-inch dimension.

## Scope and references

This list covers only modeled 2-inch square tubing. The design also uses 1-inch braces, plates, pan/tank sheet, rails and hardware. Sand adds mass/damping; it is not credited as a substitute for steel section or straight mounting datums. Weld and drill before filling any tube.
