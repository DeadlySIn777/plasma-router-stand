### FW-02 / P2 — Cabinet stringers claim predrilled clearance holes but export as plain tubes

Evidence classification: `confirmed_manufacturing_geometry_defect`.

CAB_STRINGER creation makes only the hollow rectangular section. The fixed screw stations at world Y213.4 and Y687.6 do not cut holes. Positive screw-to-stringer intersections are explicitly permitted with the reason that the holes are predrilled. **Repair:** Cut the two through-clearance holes into each stringer solid and individual export, add their local dimensions to fabrication operations, and narrow/remove the exception once the clearance is real. Retain the separate intentional self-drilling penetration in the bearer wall.

### FW-07 / P1 — Whole-frame rigidity and support stability remain unverified

Evidence classification: `structural_release_verification_missing_not_a_predicted_failure`.

The design has a real two-inch tube frame, five braces and six custom screw feet, but the supplied strength screen treats ideal members and excludes joint rotation, gusset bending and complete-frame flexibility. Lowering the front upper tie removes the front high-level brace path; the documentation itself says racking must pass through the rear/side braces and connections. The 650 kg design mass is an assumption, and the foot check is axial compression plus gross pad pressure. No complete machine load/deflection budget, assembled mass/center-of-gravity or anchorage/floor acceptance is shown. Sand is correctly not credited as added elastic stiffness.

**Repair:** Set a required tool-to-work deflection budget and actual load cases, then check the assembled frame, connections, feet and support surface with appropriate analysis and/or a measured load test. Determine mass/center of gravity for router/plasma/service configurations and close anchoring/leveling requirements. Preserve the member-screen qualification.
