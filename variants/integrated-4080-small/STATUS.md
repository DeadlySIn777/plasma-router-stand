The owner's equipment identities and earlier decisions have been recovered.
The outstanding geometric registrations are specific installed datums and
unpublished mating details. No model name needs to be asked for again.
Supplier-facing blanks remain transfer-fit faces, not invented hole patterns.

The geometry checks report unresolved cells as holds. Finite telescoping and
manual-handling samples are not called continuous proofs. The nominal source
solids cannot establish power-loss Z retention, safe head/bed handling,
controller isolation, plasma EMI performance or whole-machine rigidity.

The earlier two-bearing pan screen and earlier front-pivot collision witnesses
are historical diagnostics. The current front bay, trimmed receiver and third
pan bearer resolve those specific layout problems; do not use obsolete output
passes or failures as the current assembly status.

The earlier missing pin-retention geometry has been replaced by four recessed
stay pivot sets, two retained full-open lock pins and two inboard-headed plain
front hinge pins. Six M6 DIN937 low nuts and six 1.6x14 mm split pins retain
the stays/locks; two custom hinge washers and two 2x16 mm split pins retain
the hinges. There are no M8 hinge nuts. Their drawings, nominal local fits
and hinge service paths are explicit; material, weld, bearing, received-fit,
load capacity and supported operation still need physical qualification.

The bed fastener bearing path now includes eighty 18 OD/6.4 ID by 1.6 mm
bridge washers. Its M5x75 stock screws finish to 72.0 mm under the head;
the 65.4 mm columns and original work datums remain. Actual bearing, thread
engagement and capacity remain qualification gates, and the final proof
must bind this washer/fastener revision.

The new router rib's hole-aware ideal composite calculation gives 0.093 mm
at 100 N/50 mm tool projection, compared with 0.250 mm for the former flat
plate. Its positive dowels address the longitudinal shear path. Nominal
geometry does not prove preload, microslip, torsion or complete-machine
rigidity. The full assembly proofs must bind the final subsystem revisions.

The two owned sheets have 14 exclusive allocations: ten half-inch head
blanks and four three-eighth-inch blanks (two hinge arms, gravity ledge and
router rib). A dated partial fastener cart is recorded in
[selected-cart-evidence.json](manufacturing/selected-cart-evidence.json);
it does not establish that every BOM item is in the cart or delivered below budget.
