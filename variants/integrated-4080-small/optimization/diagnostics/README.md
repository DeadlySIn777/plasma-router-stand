# Historical transition witness

`tilt-before-rear-access-repair.json` is the failed diagnostic before the rear
dry-arm screw-head notches moved from X105/1045 to X125/1025. It records two
intersections during the first fraction of a degree of bed lift. Its old source
hashes intentionally describe that earlier geometry; it is not a current pass
or a fabrication instruction.

The current full transition result is
`bed/output/bed-machine-changeover-check.json` in the integrated variant.
The corrected D12.5 vertical access cuts open through the front and outboard
corners of the rear arms beneath the existing receiver access openings. They
are access notches, with no claimed fastening ligament or qualified section
capacity. Use the current custom STEP for the resulting profile operations.
