# Upright 4080 / single HDPE / full 20100 bed

The current bed retains ten uncut 1220 mm 20100 profiles and the full
1000 x 1220 mm profile footprint. The workplane remains Z967.8 mm.
One 1000 x 1200 x 12.7 mm HDPE rectangle covers the complete cutting area;
its front is Y-39.6 and rear Y1160.4. The profiles overhang it by 0.4 mm at
the front and 19.6 mm at the rear. HDPE provides no structural composite
credit. One nominal 48 x 48 inch raw sheet supplies this layer; even a
1212.85 mm minimum received dimension leaves 12.85 mm cleanup for the
1200 mm finished length.

The HDPE has 80 round through-holes: one 13.2 mm datum and 79 x 22 mm
clearance holes. No pockets, routed slots, counterbores or facing are used.
The profiles remain uncut and undrilled. The first/last profile anchors are
shifted inward to real bottom-slot axes X125/165 and X985/1025, avoiding
the end-core fasteners in the upright beams. The other slot axes remain
unchanged. Follow the current drill CSV/DXF rather than older two-layer
drawings.

## Stack, bearing and retention

Four 980 mm 4080 crossmembers now stand **40Y x 80Z** at
Y110/395/780/1140. Their bottom is Z855.1 mm; the HDPE bottom is Z935.1.
The pan, complete slats and their supports move down 26 mm. The fixed dry
arms/receiver seats move down 27.3 mm from the previous flat-beam version.
Receiver flanges bear on the arms at Z851.925 and seat the beams at Z855.1.
The front beam is supported through the two retained front hinge assemblies.

Eighty metal columns are **12.7 OD / 6.35 ID x 92.7 mm** nominal.
Eighty steel M6 DIN9021 bridge washers, 18 OD / 6.4 ID x 1.6 mm, positively
capture the 13.1 mm crossmember bores. They contact the crossbar undersides
and square column ends. This retains the previous repair to the omitted
crossbar retention path; a small screw head and plain column alone can
pass through the bore.

Buy M5 x 100 socket screws and finish them to **99.3 +/-0.1 mm underhead**,
with no more than 0.4 mm tip deburr/chamfer. The nominal full nut engagement
is 2.7 mm, tip extension 1.0 mm and cavity-ceiling clearance 1.1 mm.
The checked screw/washer tolerance corners retain at least 0.8 mm clearance.
Untrimmed stock is outside this checked margin. Fit column lengths to the
actual sheet stack and ensure both metal seats contact.

The upright profile's bottom groove reduces the nominal washer bearing
patch: all 80 modeled patches measure at least 77.285 mm2. This is a
geometry/contact result, not a washer, lip, torque or joint-slip capacity
rating. The actual supplied profile die, nut and material must qualify.
Receiver washer accesses are 22 mm and open the local flange edge at X125
and its mirror. The lower frame-arm head accesses remain 12.5 mm.

Two lower-core M8 x 35 rear drawdowns retain the closed bed, one per side;
together with the front two hinges these restrain all four corners.
Remove both complete lower lock sets before tilting. Two upper M8 x 20
endcap bolts and washers stay with the moving bed. Their nominal core
engagement is 12.05 mm; the lower locks retain 20.4 mm. Actual core/thread,
endcap, receiver and joint capacities remain qualification items.

The moving hinge arms are 270 x 128 x 9.525 mm, at Y-127..143.
Their original 12 mm hinge axis remains Y-100 / Z967.8. The 80 x 40 x 10 mm
spacer blanks rotate to 40Y x 80Z. End bolts align to Y110 and the two
upright core heights. The updated global owned-stock nest must allocate
these arms uniquely with the head rib and ledge; no extra owned sheet is
assumed. The steel assist brackets, including actual core-bolt head
clearances, have their own new-stock takeoff.

## Tilt assistance and pins

Use a newly selected **150 lb / 667 N per-unit gas pair**, not the former
200 lb pair. The nominal eyes travel 456.184..658.290 mm. Required received
acceptance is compressed length no more than 445 mm, extended length at
least 700 mm, body no more than 24 mm, rod no more than 10 mm and verified
ball/socket articulation. These are acceptance envelopes, not OEM CAD.

The upper gas eyes move to Y365 / Z912.4 closed, with 6.35 mm steel ears,
1.05 mm nominal spacers, M8 washers and locking nuts. The upper stay eyes
are Y395 / Z859.4. This separates their hardware from the upright beam's
two core bolts. The lower gas/stay anchors retain their previous positions.

Calculated moving deck mass is 49.25..53.64 kg across the stated 4080 area
assumptions. With the nominal gas force, the reachable-grip screen gives
97.54..138.24 N; one effective spring raises that to 252.71..293.40 N.
The rear-edge grip transfers at 35 degrees to the mid-side edge; maximum
modeled grip height is 1702 mm. These are ideal static figures. Actual
force curves, assist selfweight, handling, supported transitions and
fall control remain unqualified. Gas springs are not positive supports.

All eight pins include their retention hardware: four recessed stay
pivots, two inboard-headed front hinges and two full-open lock pins.
One new 3/4 x 12 inch steel bar yields the eight pins and four hinge rings
under the exclusive 303 mm cut plan. No raw-stock or owned-stock credit is
duplicated.

The four stay heads are recessed 0.1 mm below their 5 mm outer ears.
They retain 1.25 mm nominal rail-end clearance, or 0.75 mm at the accepted
0.5 mm outward float. M6 slotted nuts and new 1.6 x 14 split pins retain
them. Both full-open locks also receive washers, nuts and new split pins;
their cotter heads face outward above the rails. Fully retain both locks
before releasing a supported upright bed.

Front hinge heads face inward because the front post/base obstructs
outside-fed pins. With the unloaded deck externally supported, remove and
discard each straightened front-facing 2 x 16 cotter, remove its washer
6 mm outward, then withdraw the pin 26 mm inward. Capture the axial shim.
Insertion reverses those paths using a new cotter. The global machine
report checks these continuous material sweeps.

## Machine changeover and evidence

Remove the entire installed head/tool module; retain the common dock.
Lock the machine at Y1275 / X975 / Z250. Store the guide screens, park
the ATC 200 mm rearward and fit its hood. Park both complete front-stop
assemblies using their qualified transfer route. Raise the empty bed with
controlled support and fully retain both upright locks. Restore, secure
and prove both front stops before restoring guide screens or enabling
motion. Actual connectors, state sensing, handling and physical retention
still require commissioning.

The full bed remains inside the permanent frame plan and reaches
Z2247.8 mm upright. Check overhead clearance. Continuous rigid-bed geometry
and finite telescoping-assist pose checks have different scopes; finite
samples are never described as a continuous assist-motion proof.

The nominal 100 N bed compliance screen improves from 0.29624 to
0.15633 mm using an explicitly assumed strong-axis I of 536000 mm4.
At 50% of that retained I it gives 0.20296 mm; at 25% it gives 0.29624 mm.
No actual supplier section or drilled ligament has been qualified by this
calculation. Slot/joint slip, asymmetric loads, dynamic/thermal behavior
and the complete gantry/Z/spindle/tool loop are excluded. This is not a
steel-machining or whole-machine accuracy qualification.

The unchanged full-depth pan section and spans retain the 1.15605 mm
full-brim displacement screen without composite credit. Its 26 mm rigid
translation changes no slat depth or water capacity. Actual support,
weld distortion, leakage, overflow flow and thermal behavior still require
physical acceptance.
