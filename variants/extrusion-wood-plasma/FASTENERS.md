**Confirm both extrusion dies before bulk purchase.** Nominal 8 mm mouth width and an M6 thread do not guarantee room for the nut body, reliable seating on the slot lips, roll-in clearance or correct screw length. Buy/measure one sample of each used profile and candidate nut; verify the actual drawing, slot depth, thread engagement, end clearance and supplier load limits. The Walmart page also has inconsistent generic series copy, so use the size-specific dimensions, not the marketing text, for the fit comparison. Neither offer's availability in full project quantity, tax or freight to 30510 was confirmed.

**Screws:** 16 cap and 20 datum-bar flush stations need a compatible 90° flat socket head. Other stations use a projecting head where clearance permits. Bolt Depot showed [M6×16 flat](https://boltdepot.com/Product-Details?product=13280) at **$0.21 each / $11.99 per 100**; [M6×16 button](https://boltdepot.com/Product-Details?product=13351) at **$0.19 each / $10.34 per 100**; [M6×20 button](https://boltdepot.com/Product-Details?product=13352) at **$0.20 each / $11.48 per 100**. These lengths and finishes are pricing examples: select actual lengths separately for the 4 mm straps, stood-off straps, 6–6.35 mm plates, and ~8 mm flush parts. Flat head length includes the head. Verify selected head diameter/angle sits flush in the modeled Ø13 recess; budget an appropriate corrosion-resistant finish for the actual environment.
