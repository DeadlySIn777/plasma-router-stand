# Motion and tool exchange — extrusion-frame variant

Two separate working poses use the original compact machine's selected XYZ hardware. **The carving STEP contains the spindle; the plasma STEP contains the float/breakaway head in its working position.** Neither contains an invented cutting bit or a guessed AG-60 nozzle. The unbored split plasma insert is deliberate. Part files for the new lower mounting plate are a design candidate; they do not release the thermal, electrical, strength or purchased mounting interfaces.

The **47.8 mm** difference in Y axis location is real in the models: 35.1 mm from the original head geometry and 12.7 mm from the new plate. CNC work offsets and homing/soft limits must account for the chosen head. These are provisional axis-center ranges, not a promise of accessible material at every point. Torch geometry, stock, clamps, pan and overtravel must be checked separately.

**Reused hardware:** two KHMOS HMS40 1000 mm Y drives (Amazon B0C7GN24S1); one HMS40 800 mm X drive (B0C7GQTRRX); one RATTMMOTOR ZBX80 100 mm Z (B09MVYGLNQ); two 1420 mm HGR20 Y guides, two 1200 mm HGR20 X guides and eight HGH20CA blocks. The 1200 mm 8080 gantry is retained; the cheaper chassis extrusion is not silently substituted for the beam. See [recorded purchasing identities](../../output/release-review/RevE-ENGINEERING/verified-purchases.json) and [Rev I plasma hardware](../../output/design-finish-2026-09-26/motion/README.md). Stored-tool/bed hardware, original chassis/caps and ATC are not imported into this motion package. The model carries the stock Z motor size; suspended-axis retention remains unqualified.

**Connection to this chassis:** the main variant assembly uses [frame.py](frame.py) to replace the original Y datum blanks with slotted-extrusion-mounted bars. The old weld-to-steel-tube instruction is superseded. Flush screws go in before the rail. Confirm the actual rail pattern does not coincide with those screws. The existing home/stop brackets require four Ø6.6 cap holes per side at Y90, 140, 1410 and 1442; X66.5 on the left, X1083.5 on the right; cap top Z1063.9375. No HMS40 base bolts are inferred from an unknown nut cavity. Verify actual module attachment, stop contacts, cap support, flatness, block preload, motors/drives, travel endpoints, actual lead, hose/cable sweeps and the guarded Z output fitting before fabrication or commissioning. The new frame screws are not certified simply because the bars meet the profile in the model.

**Why lower the plasma head?** The old common adapter puts the bottom of the 40 mm straight grip at Z1095 even at minimum Z. Lowering the head 115 mm places that datum at Z980. A deeper 130 mm arrangement intersected the side ledger at minimum Z. The adapter is removable for carving, leaving the original router projection intact. For a specified torch, let `P` be the measured downward distance from bottom of the permitted grip to nozzle, `S` the actual slat top, `t` the sheet thickness, and `h` the cutter manufacturer's standoff. Required Z lift is `S + t + h + P − 980`; it must lie within the actual qualified Z range, with room for probing, pierce and retract. For illustration, at S=911, t=3 and h=2 mm, the target is Z916 and P must be 64–164 mm using the entire nominal travel. A hypothetical P=80 gives a Z lift of 16 mm. These are geometry examples, not torch measurements or recommended plasma settings. The body of the spindle is a separate reference: cutter protrusion, collet and real fixture determine available cutting depth.

**Plate and fasteners:** the [110 × 185 × 12.7 mm plate STEP](motion-detail/WP_PLASMA_DROP_115.step), with [DXF pilot and counterbore layers](motion-detail/WP_PLASMA_DROP_115.dxf), fits in the nominal 12 × 12 × ½ in stock. Allow for stock condition and other parts allocated to that piece. Candidate material is sound machinable aluminium of confirmed alloy; mass is about 0.69 kg at 2700 kg/m³. Four upper M6 × 1 through taps on a 90 × 26 mm rectangle receive the original adapter-side M6×16 screw size. The lower pattern is 115 mm below it: four Ø6.6 through holes, Ø10.5 × 6 mm rear recesses, with four additional M6×16 screws into the custom plasma backplate. Both sets have 9.3 mm nominal engagement past a 6.7 mm web. This is **8 screws total on the plasma tool-side assembly**, separate from the as-delivered Z-stage fasteners still to be established. The head-facing opening in the insert requires an actual barrel record. Assemble the lower head to the plate on the bench; the upper screws enter from the rear of the common adapter, so do not assume in-place access behind the Z carriage. Final fastener selection, tightening, access and lead/impact loads require verification. The additional moving mass matters for Z retention and acceleration.

[Motion evidence](motion-evidence.json) checks both modes' individual solid validity and verifies their axis locations. The [new-interface clearance check](motion-detail/clearance-check.json) rebuilt the center and all eight X/Y/Z travel corners. It tested positive-volume interference of the lowered head and new parts against selected motion solids at those nine poses, excluding existing head internals and pre-existing common/common interfaces. No unclassified intersections exceeded 0.02 mm³. This is not a continuous sweep, completed chassis integration test or an operating release. No cables, cutting tip or energized torch test are represented. Complete the actual Z/brake/terminal interfaces and mode-specific validation before use.

The [separate frame-and-bed travel check](motion-detail/external-envelope.json) includes both installed modes, including the four upright steel bridges in the plasma rack. It computes conservative swept axis-aligned boxes for the nominal translated parts and widens the cam-operated roller bounds. It also tests actual solid intersections at 11 poses, including mid-travel with the head low at each side. Neither mode has a positive swept-box overlap against the external chassis or its mode-specific bed. The smallest nominal box separation is **1.0 mm** (Y guide block to cap); the lowest spindle body has **1.2 mm** over the bare wood backer; the lowered plasma head has **6.5 mm** to a ledger locating pin. Moving hardware stays at least **32 mm** from the rack/stored panels under these assumptions. These are geometry clearances before assembly error, deflection, workpieces, clamps or leads. The check pertains to fully installed states and seated head, not transfer, probing float, docking failure or physical stop overshoot. Confirm rail height, cap flushness, homing and clearances over travel on the assembled machine before setting working limits.
