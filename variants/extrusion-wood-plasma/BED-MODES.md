# One fixed plasma pan, four lift-out carving bridges

The steel pan stays on three frame crossmembers in both modes. For wood jobs, four metal bridge panels span between two separate, dry steel ledgers on the frame. Each bridge has two formed edge ribs, an underside 25.4 mm square center rib, four hold-downs and a round/relieved locating-hole pair. The woodworking load reaches the frame through those side ledgers, not through the slats or sheet-metal pan. In plasma mode the bridges stand in a comb rack behind the pan **inside the footprint**. No wood panel, dust boot, or wood stock lives under the active torch.

**Wood fixturing.** Four M6 fasteners secure each metal bridge to its ledgers. The round locator plus an X-relieved slot gives each bridge a return datum; actual fits and reinstallation accuracy need measurement. Each backer shows six countersunk clearance holes over M6 weld nuts. Use flush fasteners and reserve a no-cut area around them in CAM: X190, 575, 960 crossed with Y = front edge of that panel + 33 and +224 mm. Panel fronts are Y103.4, 362.4, 621.4 and 880.4. The required radius includes the actual screw head, cutter radius and setup error. Set the tool depth to preserve material over the metal at Z946.1, and allow for prior surfacing. The backers' holes do not by themselves clamp the workpiece. Select screws, clamps and locating stops for the wood job; determine stock fit and collision limits with the actual fixtures. No metal milling is proposed.

**Removable pan retention.** Gravity loads the three sloped saddles. Four separate clamp brackets wrap the front/rear crossbearers: each upper strap and lower jaw sandwich the 4040 with two M6 through screws **outside** the extrusion, tightened from below into captive threaded bosses. Reliefs in the two outer steel saddles clear the upper straps. No holes or modified T-slots are needed in the profiles. Each bracket carries a boss, Ø8 shoulder and detachable keeper. A steel ear welds to the **outside** of the pan wall without a fluid penetration; the shoulder passes through the ear and the cap screw tightens against its sleeve, not the pan. The front-left Ø8.6 hole locates XY, the front-right 8.6 × 12.6 obround restrains Y/yaw and allows ±2.3 mm in X. Both Ø14 rear holes allow 3 mm radial differential motion. All four caps capture uplift after 1 mm travel. Measure and verify that clearances stay positive at the real installation temperatures. The pockets are outside the wet area and do not obstruct the slats, drain or overflow. Disconnect plumbing and remove the four caps before planned pan removal; the heavy pan needs a separate supported handling procedure.

**The manual swap.** Unload stock and backers; remove cutter, dust boot and dust hose. Cool, clean and drain the pan as appropriate. Before reaching into the machine, park the bare spindle at X575 / gantry Y275 / Z lift 100 and isolate motion and tool power with Z mechanically retained. The rack is behind the bed, not in front of it. Open its four retained swivel keepers. Numbering panels front-to-rear 1–4:

1. Release the four hold-downs of panel **4**. Lift it flat until its top reaches Z1008 (about 62 mm). Slide it rearward until its front top edge is at Y1080.
2. Tilt the rear edge upward 90° about that front edge, with hands supporting the 4.89 kg bridge. Shift the now-vertical bridge to slot Y1354 and lower its edge to the Z714 rests.
3. Repeat for panels 3, 2 and 1, to slots Y1308, 1262 and 1216 respectively. Tighten all four swivel keepers after seating. Only cleaned metal bridges are stored there; remove wood waste and the backers from the hot-work area.
4. Fit the separate torch head and lead with power isolated; tool exchange and physical lead handling need their own validation. For wood restoration, re-establish the same parked bare-spindle condition before reversing the panel transfers. Cool and inspect the pan/slats, drain and dry the carving setup, reseat and tighten bridges, fit the job backers and recheck wood zero.

The modeled panel sweeps stay inside plan bounds: no cart, hoist, or full-bed pullout. The highest panel edge is about Z1265 during tilting. This is a kinematic study without hands, installed hoses, an actual nozzle, dirt, shop-floor access or physical lifting trials. Keep room for the operator; an in-footprint panel path does not imply no operator aisle.

**Drain and fill.** The floor falls 0.6% toward the front steel wall. A real Ø24 mm bore connects to the face-mounted drain; its invert at Z847 leaves approximately 2 mm at the low end, so a drain command does not mean dry. Comb cutouts pass water between bays. A separate 40 × 8 mm overflow connects at Z896. Allow a removable coarse basket/cleanout, accessible valve, continuous gravity return and a permanently vented receiver sized for the **whole measured inventory**, hose contents, sludge displacement and freeboard. For automatic transfer, use a pump to refill from the receiver, a level stop, independent high-high shutdown, low-reservoir protection and a verified passive overflow; inhibit both tools during transfer. Size and prove drain/overflow flow against the chosen pump, inspect for blockage, calibrate wet, and physically prove mode interlocks. Valve, strainer, hose, air gap, reservoir, pump and electrical logic **are not supplied in these bed solids**. Do not apply the compressor or venturi to a homemade tank. The original [water arrangement](../../output/release-review/RevE-ENGINEERING/WATER-CONTROL.md) is a functional reference, not transferable dimensions or capacities.

**Plasma isolation.** Specify plain uncoated mild-steel sheet as this variant's proposed plasma material. Protect the aluminum, slots, rails, wiring and parked equipment from spatter. The two fixed steel side skirts protect the lower side interfaces; they do not enclose the upper guides, operators or surroundings. The proposed operating boundary does not allow the torch to cut the aluminum chassis. Prove independent fume capture before live operation, with the wood dust hose physically disconnected and its collector isolated. Inspect and remove dust from slots, tray, rack and nearby surfaces before hot work; do not blow it into the air with compressed air. Work lead on clean retained steel, separate protective bonding/EMI measures following the exact cutter manual; do not depend on anodized profiles or motion bearings to carry cutting current. Confirm actual torch, start circuit, documented CNC interface and safe interlocks for the owner-reported [VIV ARC CUT-50](../../output/release-review/RevE-ENGINEERING/PLASMA-COMPATIBILITY.md). No live-plasma permission, metal thickness rating or CNC electrical pinout is implied. See [OSHA wood dust and fire guidance](https://www.osha.gov/etools/woodworking/production/fire-explosion), [Hypertherm's water-table aluminum guidance](https://www.hypertherm.com/resources/faqs/is-it-okay-to-plasma-cut-aluminum-on-a-water-table/) and [manufacturer fume guidance](https://xnet.hypertherm.com/Xnet/library/library.jsp?file=HYP104686).

**Geometry and limits.** [Bed checks](bed-check.json) and the [reproduction script](check_process_bed.py) check the new bed internally in both end states, sample each panel transfer against the actual front-parked motion, frame, fixed bed and other bridges, and calculate water volume after submerged slat/comb displacement. For each pan mount, exact solids demonstrate pan-ear contact with the dry wall, strap contact with the unmodified bearer, intended free motion, and physical stops on overtravel/uplift. Nominal male threads against minor-diameter CAD bores are identified separately. Endpoint solids have valid volumes. Transfer sampling is not a continuous clearance proof or a safe operating test. The side-ledger and rack brackets use frame-slot nuts; confirm slot family, hardware engagement, pullout and wet-load capacity with the **actual** extrusions. The four pan mounts are integrated; their weld/ear strength, fastener preload, clamp sliding capacity along the extrusion, local profile-wall crushing, tolerances and hot working clearance require qualification. Likewise verify local pan sheet stiffness, fluid-tightness, steel/aluminum corrosion, protective bonding, splash capture and overflow performance. Thread forms, bend radii and fabrication drawings remain outside this package.
