## Fit and fastening purchases

Both listings identify 6063-T5 and a nominal 40 mm square envelope. VEVOR gives a Ø6.8 mm center bore and lists M6 and M8 T-nut thread options. Its [manual, 4040 drawing](https://d37keo26p536wj.cloudfront.net/mdm-goods-service-prod/%EF%BC%88SKU1~12%EF%BC%89AluminiumExtrusionLinearRail%EF%BC%88M%EF%BC%89_1738806572120.pdf) shows the internal slot geometry. The product pages do **not** establish identical slot cavities, exact section inertia or rated joint forces for both brands. The CAD represents stock envelopes; the simplified grooves are not dimensions for machining nuts.

The current fabricated connection plates and straps use nominal **M6 clearance**. Verify a sample M6 nut in **each** profile: it must seat across both lips, resist rotation and obtain sufficient thread engagement without bottoming. The bolt length depends on plate/spacer thickness. Don't buy a mixed box of M8 fasteners for modeled Ø6.6 holes.

For a price reference only, [B0BNBP7SFP on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BNBP7SFP) showed **$14.99 per 10 gussets**, with 20 M6 × 16 screws and 20 nuts advertised for an 8 mm slot. The listing does not rate this joint for the machine's loads. The kit is **not in the $319.88, not a specified replacement for the drawn connection plates and not a complete fastener takeoff**. Feet, shelf plates, load-distributing plates and motion datum strips are separate purchases/fabrication.
