# Optional automatic water transfer — open tank, gravity out, pump back

The [water option STEP](review/WATER.step) is an overlay at the same coordinates as WOOD.step and PLASMA.step. It fits the six- and nine-foot frame layouts without moving their beds or supporting the reservoir from the machine. This is a plumbing **layout and control sequence for review**, not commissioned equipment or fabrication drawings. The extrusion price does not include it.

**Water path:** An independent floor reservoir supplies the fixed water pan through a low-voltage submersible pump and a normally closed fill valve. A return spout discharges into the pan at Z939, **32 mm above its Z907 rim**. A second, normally closed full-port valve drains the pan by gravity. The existing front scupper has a **separate, continuously open overflow return**. Nothing pressurizes or evacuates the tank; the user's compressor, venturi and unverified air solenoids are not water-handling components.

**Front connections.** Two hollow welded drop-box allowances meet the pan's existing OD28/ID24 round drain and 40 × 8 mm overflow opening at Y30. The gravity line (ID20) and independent overflow line (ID22) continuously descend to the vented reservoir. Their free ends are at Z314—above tank high-high. The pump uses a separate ID12 route up the front before discharging above the pan. The closest new plumbing reaches Y4, still within the front of the footprint. Keep exposed components near the pan noncombustible; support and shield the plumbing and valve wiring from sparks, spray and vibration. The shown fittings and valves are allocations, with no fabricated sealing joint, vendor threads, clamp or support spacing selected.

**Service and sizing.** The removable pump and strainer occupy the footprint below the service hatch. The model checks 110 mm of clear vertical space for the lid leaves and their across-hatch stacking motion. This is an in-place service arrangement, not a demonstrated path to remove a tank filled with water. The 2 mm shell, runners, seams, clips, pump supports and couplings still need fabrication details, a leak test and load checks for the actual stock. Fit a removable inlet screen/coarse basket and a cleanout suited to metal grit; guard the pump and keep the overflow independently clear. An overflow into the same tank does not contain a stand-alone tank leak.

Select a pump compatible with the actual water mixture, particles and minimum immersion, with dry-run protection and a manufacturer curve at **about 0.9 m static rise plus the losses** of the selected tubing, bends and fill valve. A design target of 3–5 L/min at that actual duty would refill the nominal pan in roughly **8–13 minutes**. It is not a promised flow or a measured cycle time. Prove the passive scupper's capacity with the real dirty-water hardware at maximum pump output and loss of primary level sensing. If the overflow cannot carry the flow below the rim, reduce/limit the delivered flow or change the scupper and recheck. Both selected valves must operate at **zero differential pressure** and fail closed; ordinary pilot-operated airline valves may not work on gravity water.

|---|---|
| Switch to plasma | Water transfer runs with both tools inhibited. After the wood, dust hose and spoilboards have been removed, the metal bridges secured in their rack, and the work area cleaned, the operator requests fill. Prove drain closed and tank above its low cutoff. Open NC fill valve; start pump only after open feedback. Stop pump and close fill valve at pan work level Z887. No automatic torch start. |
| Switch to wood | With cutting disabled and fill verified closed, accept a drain request only if the tank has starting headroom. Open NC gravity drain. After pan-low Z851 clears, wait for independently validated drain-tail/flow completion before closing. A dry low probe alone does not prove that the slats, pan or shop are clean and dry. Inspect, clean and dry before installing wood and reconnecting dust collection. |
| Stuck valve, bad feedback, pump low-water, timeout, pan high-high Z893, tank high-high Z292 or loss of power/isolation | Stop pump; de-energize both NC valves and latch a fault. Overflow and tank vent remain passive. Reset does not start a cycle; a new operator request is required. A mechanically stuck valve is not made safe by removing its coil power—verify feedback/flow and isolate for service. |

The [Python model](water_option.py) implements the sequence as a review function only. It does not control an MCU, energize a relay or grant cutting permission. Noncontact or appropriately protected dirty-water level sensing, actual valve-position feedback, pump-dry-run protection and fail-safe cut interlocks remain engineering work. Make the low/headroom/high levels, blocked-flow detection and timeout thresholds repeatable in a **cold water-only** commissioning test before relying on automatic switching.

The [verification record](review/water-option.json) includes measured capacities, state-sequence examples, STEP re-import, and solid interference checks against the 9-foot frame, both bed modes and both exported machine poses. The diagrammed pipes establish a potential route, not complete hose bend/droop clearance, dynamic head/torch/stock clearance or leak/overflow qualification. No pump/valve/float ASIN is specified or included in the $319.88/$379.78 extrusion figures: the actual head curve, seal and solids compatibility, gravity-valve bore, feedback and port dimensions must match before parts are priced as an operating set.
