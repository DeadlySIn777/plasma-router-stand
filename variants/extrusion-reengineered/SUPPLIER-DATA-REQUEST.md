## RapidChange magazine

Please provide the current STEP or dimensioned mounting template for the
intended ER11/ER16/ER20-family linear magazine, including its exact model
and revision. The public FAQ identifies a 60 mm family, while the actual
mounting models are distributed through the vendor's Discord channel.

Required fields:

- Complete base, end-cap and cover envelopes in both cover positions.
- Tool-pocket center coordinates and mounting-hole diameters, threads/depths
  and locations, all relative to a defined mounting face.
- The mounting-face-to-nut/engage plane and required spindle engagement motion.
- Retained tool projection below the base, maximum tool diameter, nut/collet
  compatibility and any approved mounting riser/recess arrangement.
- Sensor/servo connection requirements and supported spindle/VFD sequence.

The current carrier has replaceable mounting blanks and a declared supplier
acceptance volume. They are not a substitute for this actual hole template.
Confirm the user's real spindle and nut before selecting a socket variant.

## RATTMMOTOR ZXHT80-200 / B09MYD6Z23

Please supply the full mechanical drawing for the exact 200 mm stroke variant,
including the M5 bottom-slot/nut mounting, output fixing pattern, all thread
depths and any locating pilots. The preserved public drawings establish a
319 mm body and 430 mm stock-motor package, but omit these mating details.

For the motor/coupling, please confirm:

- Actual front flange, pilot, bolt pattern and shaft diameter/projection.
- Screw-end coupling shaft diameter and the installed coupling specification.
- Whether a compatible factory power-off brake option is available.
- Whether the documented STEPPERONLINE 23HS22-2804D-B200 brake-motor candidate
  can preserve the front datum, or which approved motor/coupling is required.
- Actual screw lead, motor current, vertical-use limits and retention procedure.

The brake candidate's package and static rating are documented separately.
Compatibility, engagement delay and dynamic stopping are not presumed.
