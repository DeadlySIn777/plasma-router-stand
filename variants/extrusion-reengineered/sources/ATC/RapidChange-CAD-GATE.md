# RapidChange magazine CAD source gate

The [official FAQ](https://rapidchangeatc.com/faq/) confirms the current 60 mm body family and shared ER11/ER16/ER20 body geometry. It directs mounting models to a [Discord downloads channel](https://discord.com/channels/1077383334080032898/1191144591840268318). That page yielded no unauthenticated attachment here; no server membership or account changes were made.

The bounded search also checked all seven public repositories under the manufacturer's Greilick Industries organization, plus public magazine/STEP media searches. No usable mechanical model or mounting template was found. This is a vendor-data/access gate, not proof that no public file exists.

Pocket centres, mounting holes, engagement plane and selected magazine envelope remain unverified. The product page's 520 × 120 × 120 mm generic dimensions are not a mechanical drawing. Keep the carrier's 520 × 60 × 80 mm allowance explicitly labeled as a design acceptance volume until an authorized current model or measured unit closes the interface. No photo scaling or old 54 mm model was used.
